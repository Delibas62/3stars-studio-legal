## Common questions

**Do I need an account or an internet connection?**
No. The game plays fully offline from the first launch, and no account is needed. Online features (the global leaderboard and cloud backup) are optional.

**How do I keep my empire when I change phones?**
Open **Profile → Cloud Save** and either *Sign in with Apple* (iPhone) or *Link Google account*. Your progress is then backed up to the cloud automatically. On the new phone, sign in the same way and your empire comes back. You can also tap *Back up* and *Restore* yourself.

**I bought "No Ads — Instant Rewards" or "Tycoon Premium" but it isn't active.**
Open **Profile** and tap **Restore purchases**. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account (iPhone) or Google account (Android) you bought with. If it still does not work, email us with the date of the purchase.

**How do I cancel Tycoon Premium?**
- iPhone: **Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Empire Ledger**.
- Android: **Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions**.
Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid the next charge. Premium stays active until the paid period ends.

**How do I get a refund?**
Refunds are handled by the store you bought from. On iPhone, request one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). On Android, follow Google Play's refund process.

**Someone on the leaderboard has an offensive name.**
Hold that player's row on the leaderboard to **report** the name or **hide** the player from your board. Names are also filtered for offensive words automatically.

**How do I delete my data?**
In the game: **Profile → Cloud Save → Delete my cloud data**. Without the game installed: see our [Account & Data Deletion page](./delete-account).

**Is any real money involved in the stock or crypto markets?**
No. Empire Ledger is a simulation. All companies, coins and properties are fictional, and nothing in the game can be cashed out.
