# Privacy Policy — Empire Ledger: Business Tycoon

**Developer:** 3StarStudio (independent developer)
**Contact:** 3starsstudio@gmail.com
**Effective date:** September 13, 2026
**Last updated:** September 13, 2026

This policy applies to the mobile application **Empire Ledger: Business Tycoon** ("the App") published on Google Play by **3StarStudio** ("we", "us"). The App is an offline-first idle business-tycoon game. This policy explains what information the App handles, why, and the choices you have. We designed the App to collect as little as possible: **no account sign-up, no email, no real name, no location, no contacts, no camera or microphone access.**

---

## 1. Information we collect

### 1.1 Information stored only on your device
- **Game progress** (your balance, businesses, investments, achievements, settings). This is saved locally on your device and never leaves it unless you explicitly use *Cloud Backup* (see 1.2).
- **App preferences** (e.g. sound/haptics settings, notification opt-in).

### 1.2 Information sent to our servers (optional features)
The App uses **Google Firebase** (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) for its online features. These features are optional and only run when you use them.

| Feature | Data | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Anonymous identity** | A random, anonymous user ID generated by Firebase Authentication. No email, phone number or password is involved. | To tie your cloud backup and leaderboard entry to your device without an account. |
| **Cloud Backup** (you tap *Back up*) | Your game save data and a timestamp. | So you can restore your progress on a new or reset device. |
| **Global Leaderboard** (you tap *Join* / *Update*) | The **display name you choose** (max 24 characters) and your in-game **net-worth score**, plus a timestamp. | To show public rankings. Your display name and score are visible to other players. Please do not use your real name or other personal information as your display name. |

We do **not** collect your email address, real name, precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers for tracking.

### 1.3 Information collected automatically by service providers
Like most internet services, Firebase may temporarily process your **IP address** (used by Firebase Authentication for security and abuse prevention) and basic **technical information** — the Firebase "user agent" (SDK and operating-system versions), the app's Firebase App ID, and your anonymous user ID attached to each request — to deliver the service securely. We do not use this information to identify you personally. Google's handling of this data is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Firebase Privacy and Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

### 1.4 Analytics and crash reporting
The App currently does **not** include third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

## 2. Advertising (when enabled)
Future versions of the App may show advertisements through **Google AdMob** to keep the game free. If ads are enabled:
- AdMob may collect the **advertising ID** of your device, IP address, and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads, and may use it for personalization where permitted.
- You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (**Settings → Google → Ads → "Delete advertising ID"** or **"Opt out of Ads Personalization"**, depending on your Android version).
- Where required by law (e.g. EU/EEA/UK), you will be shown a consent prompt before personalized ads are served.
- Rewarded ads are always optional and only shown when you choose to watch them.

See Google's [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## 4. How we use information
We use the information described above only to:
- run the App and its optional online features (backup, leaderboard);
- restore your purchases and, where enabled, show ads;
- keep the service secure and prevent cheating or abuse;
- comply with legal obligations.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do not use it for profiling unrelated to the App.

## 6. Data retention
- **Local data** stays on your device until you delete the App or clear its data.
- **Cloud backup and leaderboard data** are kept until you delete them from within the App (see Section 7) or until we remove inactive data.
- Data held by service providers is retained under their own policies (linked above).

## 7. Your choices and rights

### 7.1 Deleting your account and data
Your anonymous cloud identity, together with its backup and leaderboard entry, is the only "account" the App creates. You can delete it at any time, in two ways:

- **In the App:** open **Profile → Cloud Save → Delete my cloud data**. This permanently deletes your cloud backup, your leaderboard entry, and your anonymous identity from our servers, immediately and irreversibly.
- **Outside the App (if you no longer have it installed):** visit our **[Account & Data Deletion page](./delete-account)** or email **3starsstudio@gmail.com** with the subject "Delete my Empire Ledger data" and your leaderboard display name. We will delete the matching records within 30 days and confirm by email.

Deleting your cloud data does not remove the game progress stored locally on your device.

### 7.2 Other choices
- **Delete local data:** Uninstall the App or clear its storage in Android settings.
- **Ads personalization:** Control it in your device settings (Section 2).
- **Access / correction / erasure requests:** Because the App has no accounts, we cannot link data to you by name. If you have a request, contact us at **3starsstudio@gmail.com** and we will help within 30 days.

Depending on where you live (e.g. EU/EEA under GDPR, UK, California under CCPA/CPRA, Türkiye under KVKK), you may have additional rights such as access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

## 8. Children
The App is a general-audience business simulation and is **not directed to children under 13** (or the minimum age required in your country).
